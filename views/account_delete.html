Account

Delete your account

Deletion is immediate. Your identity is erased the moment you confirm; nothing is held for a waiting period.

Delete it yourself

{{if .User}}

You are signed in as {{.User.Login}}. Go to Settings, open Delete account, and type your username to confirm.

{{else}}

Sign in, then go to Settings → Delete account and type your username to confirm. Sign in if you have not already.

{{end}}

In the Android app the same action lives under Settings (account icon) → Delete account. It asks you to type your username to confirm and calls the same endpoint as the web Settings page.

This page is the public web deletion page. If you cannot sign in, use the contact route below.

Google Play

Google Play requires an in-app account deletion path and a public web page. Both are provided: the Android app's Settings → Delete account, and this page. There is no separate retention period; deletion is immediate.

Your GDPR erasure right

This is your right to erasure under Article 17 of the GDPR. Confirmation is immediate: once you type your username and confirm, your identity and linked data are erased without a waiting period. If you want a copy first, use the data export. For erasure requests you cannot complete while signed in, use the contact route below.

The shared demo account

The public demo account contains only generated sample data. Deleting it resets it to the sample state so the public demo remains available for the next visitor. It does not contain the personal data of the person reviewing or trying the demo.

What is removed

  • Your GitHub/Forgejo login, display name, avatar, numeric provider IDs and any OIDC subject, email or groups link.
  • Your stored GitHub and Forgejo OAuth access tokens. GitLens asks each provider to revoke them and always deletes the stored copy.
  • Your stored API tokens (hashes and metadata), every browser session, and your server-side session rows.
  • Your tracked repository list and the commit, pull-request, release, workflow and metric history derived from those repositories for your account.
  • Your preferences, including sync interval, e-ink mode and Umami settings.

What stays

Repository data that another GitLens account also tracks stays with that account. Aggregate metrics or history that cannot be linked back to you may also remain. Your provider-side repositories are not touched: deleting GitLens does not delete anything on GitHub or Forgejo.

Backups taken before the deletion may still contain the old data until they rotate out; they are not used to restore an individual account.

If you are the last admin

GitLens refuses to delete the only administrator, because that would leave nobody able to manage the instance. Promote or create another administrator first, then delete this account.

The full detail is in the privacy policy.
